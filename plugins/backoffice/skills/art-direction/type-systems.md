# Type systems: a library of OPTIONS

Rules of the team still apply: at most 2 families, confirm the font is free for commercial use, prefer Google Fonts via `next/font` (self-hosted, no remote stylesheet). Entries sourced from `fontshare` are free for commercial use but load from a remote CDN by default: download and self-host them, or choose the Google stand-in. `standsInFor` names the paid faces the original studied sites used.
